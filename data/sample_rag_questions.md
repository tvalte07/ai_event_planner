# Sample questions for the RAG demo

Upload the three sample PDFs from `data/uploads/`, select **Index uploaded documents**, then try questions like these. The answer should be based on text in the matching document and name its source file.

## Venue brief

- What capacity does the sample community hall list?
- What should I confirm before booking an outdoor lawn?
- Does the venue brief provide actual prices or availability?

## Catering menu

- What vegetarian main meal ideas are listed?
- What should I ask about allergies and cross-contact?
- Does the sample menu include real catering prices?

## Decoration guide

- What does the minimal floral style recommend for table arrangements?
- What should I check for the photo backdrop and cables?
- Which decoration materials does the guide suggest reusing?

## Grounding check

- What is the sample venue's phone number?

The expected response to the last question is that the uploaded documents do not contain that information.
